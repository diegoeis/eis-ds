/05 · FAQ

Questions.

Does it work without signal?

Yes. Fieldkit stores records on the phone and syncs them when the connection returns.

Can we keep our paper checklists?

Type them in once as templates. Fieldkit keeps the same order and wording.

Who owns the data?

You do. Export everything as CSV at any time, also after you cancel.
